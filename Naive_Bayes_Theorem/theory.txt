It uses the conditional probability as the concept which well understand by using an example

Suppose you have 2 bags (B1 and B2), and in B1 we have 3 red and 5 black balls
and in B2 we have 5red and 4 black balls
Now if we are given that we took out a ball and it was red then what would be the probability
that it was taken out from the Bag B1.
The above stated eg was a perfect example of the conditional probability, here the events are 
not independent

The problem would be represented mathematically like:
                        P(B1|red)


                        and we have a formula to solve it i.e.
                        P(A|B) = P(B|A) * P(A)
                                 _____________
                                      P(B)


                        but if we have multiple factors then:
                        P(A|B1, B2, B3, B4...Bn) = P(B1|A) * P(B2|A) * P(B3|A)... * P(A)
                                                   ______________________________________
                                                    P(B1) * P(B2) * P(B3) * P(B4)...P(Bn)
