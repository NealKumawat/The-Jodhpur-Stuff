SVM: Support Vector Machine
Used for both classification and regression (type of supervised learning algorithm)
Usually it has a line or plane or something which divides the two classes accordingly

We'll understand it by just knowing the terms stated below:
    -> Hyperplane: It is a that decision boundary which divides the data.
                   It is the line(in 2d), plane(in 3d) and Hyperplane in higher dimensions

    -> Support Vectors: The data points closest to the Hyperplane

    -> Margin: The parallel line which passes through SVs both the sides.
               The higher the margin the better the classification is.
